# Code of conduct

## The standard

Treat contributors as people doing careful work in public. Disagreement about a classification, quote, or method is normal. Personal attacks, harassment, threats, stalking, deliberate intimidation, and publishing somebody else's private information are not.

Challenge the claim with a pinned source. Do not speculate about another person's ability, motives, identity, or use of tools.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock a thread, reject a contribution, or block an account when conduct makes the project unsafe or impossible to maintain.

Use GitHub's Report content action for abuse or sensitive conduct problems. Do not open a public issue containing private information. For an immediate threat, contact the appropriate local authority as well as GitHub.

Good faith measurement mistakes are not conduct violations. Repeatedly targeting a person or continuing after a moderator asks you to stop may become one.
